# Pottery Making Photographs

Place your original photographs showing the steps of making pottery here.

### Suggested Stages:
1. `01-clay-gathering.jpg` — Digging and selecting silt/clay from local ponds or riverbanks.
2. `02-clay-preparation.jpg` — Kneading, pugging, removing pebbles, mixing sand/temper.
3. `03-wheel-throwing.jpg` — Shaping the vessel on the wheel (chak).
4. `04-paddle-beating.jpg` — Expanding and shaping walls with wooden paddle (thapa) and stone anvil (pindi).
5. `05-slip-and-decoration.jpg` — Applying natural red/black mineral slips, incising lines.
6. `06-sun-drying.jpg` — Drying vessels under shade and sunlight.
7. `07-kiln-firing.jpg` — Stacking pots in the open clamp (aawa) and firing with husks/wood.

**Recommended formats**: JPG / WebP, 4:3 or 16:9 aspect ratio, 1200px to 1920px wide.
